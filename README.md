Entry for the make.fun competition "Midnight Fridge Roast": a one-line roast of a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight, 1–200 characters
`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating the gate first, then reading
the file back to verify the written bytes match.

> Midnight, hungry, and my fridge just stands there locked — the only thing in this house with boundaries.

Why this entry: The roast lands exactly on the brief: a smart fridge refusing to open after midnight. The punchline turns the petty midnight rule into the joke itself — the fridge is the only thing in the house with boundaries, so the rule, not the hunger, is the target. One line, tight, spoken like something said out loud at the fridge door at midnight.
